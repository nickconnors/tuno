Tuno: IPTV Player

A player for your own IPTV playlist, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Add the playlist your TV provider gave you, as an Xtream Codes login or an M3U link, and watch its live channels with a TV guide, its movies and its shows.

  • Live TV by category, with what's on now and next, and a full guide grid
  • Movies and TV shows as posters, with Continue Watching and My List
  • One search for channels, movies and shows
  • Favorites, Picture in Picture, and downloads to watch without the internet

Tuno comes with no content

Tuno is a player. It has no channels, movies or shows of its own, sells no subscriptions and is not connected to any provider. What you watch comes from the playlist you add, and you need to have the right to watch it.

No playlist yet? Enter demo as the server on the Add Playlist screen to look around with sample content built into the app.
