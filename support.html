Support

Tuno is a player for your own IPTV playlist. It comes with no channels or content, and has no accounts of its own.

Adding your playlist

Your TV provider gives you one of two things:

  • An Xtream Codes login: a server address (it looks like http://provider.com:8080), a username and a password. This brings live channels with categories and a guide, and the provider's movies and shows.
  • An M3U link: one long link to a playlist file. Paste it under M3U URL.

If you have a long link with get.php in it, paste it into Server: the username and password fill in by themselves.

Looking around without a playlist

On the Add Playlist screen, enter demo as the server and press Add Playlist. No username or password is needed. This loads sample content built into the app: invented channels with a guide, movies and shows, all playing one short test video. Remove it in Settings when you add your own playlist.

Common questions

  • A channel doesn't play. Channels come from your provider. Try another channel, check your connection, and ask your provider if a channel stays down. Settings → Playback has a stream format switch (TS or HLS) worth trying.
  • "The login is incorrect" or "expired". That is your provider's answer. Check the details with them. To enter them again: Settings, swipe right on the playlist.
  • No TV guide on some channels. A guide comes from your provider, and many cover only some channels. You can add another guide link in Settings → TV Guide.
  • Downloads. Movies and episodes from your playlist can be saved to watch without the internet, when your provider's files allow it. Settings → Downloads has Use Mobile Data and the space used.
  • Removing everything. Settings → swipe left on a playlist → Remove. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.
